Sliding Puzzle

Level: 1/300 Grid: 3x3 Difficulty: Easy

Arrange the tiles in numerical order by sliding them into the empty space.

You have 5 shuffles per level! Use them wisely.

Note: Replaying completed levels won't add to your score.

Moves: 0
Score: 0

How to Play

1. The goal is to arrange the tiles in numerical order from 1 to N.

2. Click on a tile adjacent to the empty space to slide it into that space.

3. Continue sliding tiles until all numbers are in order.

4. You have 5 shuffles per level. Use them wisely!

5. Try to complete the puzzle in as few moves as possible!

6. The game has 300 levels with increasing difficulty.

7. Higher levels have larger grids (up to 8x8).

8. For image puzzles, the image is divided into numbered tiles that work just like the number puzzles.

9. Important: Replaying already completed levels won't add to your total score.

Score System

  • Regular Levels: Base score = Grid Rows - 2 (minimum 1 point)
  • Custom Puzzles: Fixed scores based on grid size
  • Speed Bonus: +1 point if completed in less than 12 moves
  • No-Shuffle Bonus: +1 point if you don't use any shuffle
  • No replay points: Replaying completed levels gives no score

Example: 4x4 level completed in 10 moves with no shuffle used:

  • Base: 2 points (4 rows - 2)
  • Speed bonus: +1 (completed in less than 12 moves)
  • No-shuffle bonus: +1 (no shuffle used)
  • Total: 4 points
