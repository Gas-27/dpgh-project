How to Play

1
Move your mouse (or drag the joystick on mobile) to steer your snake toward the cursor.
2
Eat the glowing food scattered across the arena to grow longer and earn points.
3
Hold Space (or the BOOST button) to speed up. Boosting drains energy — it recharges automatically.
4
Cut off opponents. If an enemy runs into your body they explode into food — collect it all!
5
Never hit another snake's body or the arena boundary — you die instantly.
6
Pick a map from the menu for different visuals and food colours. Customise your skin in Setup.
