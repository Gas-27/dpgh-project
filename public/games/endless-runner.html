How to Play

  • Jump — ↑ / W / Space / right side of screen. Double jump: tap jump again in the air!
  • Slide — ↓ / S / left side of screen (hold to keep sliding)
  • Tall hanging walls can only be passed by sliding underneath
  • You owe the Boss money and he is chasing you! Collect coins and meet friends to pay the debt
  • Rooftop gaps — jump across them (double jump helps)
  • Market: jump over shoppers and dogs
  • Travel the world: every country is a new run. Neighbours are reached by car (change lanes with ↑ ↓, ← → brake/boost), far ones by plane (hold ↑ ↓ to fly). Travel costs add to your debt, and the Boss adds his bill too!
  • Jump over crates, barriers and birds
  • You have 3 lives — life pickups are rare
  • Score = 1 point every 10 seconds + 3 per coin
  • Shield · Slow-Mo · Magnet · Life
