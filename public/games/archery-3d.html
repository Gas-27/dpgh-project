ARCHERY 3D

Clear all 20 stages, earn stars and coins and upgrade your bow along the way.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Hold to draw the bow. Drag (or move the mouse) to aim. Release to shoot. When the crosshair glows gold, release for a PERFECT shot (+25%).
  2. There are 20 stages. The top-left of the screen shows how many are left to complete.
  3. Every stage has a goal of points and a limited number of arrows. Reach the goal to clear the stage. Keep scoring to earn up to 3 stars.
  4. Target stages: hit the rings (bullseye is 10). Arrows drop and the wind pushes them, so aim above the target and into the wind. Watch the flag.
  5. Hunt stages: animals roam the open field. Aim ahead of where they are walking. A shot behind the shoulder is a CLEAN SHOT worth double. Rabbits are small and fast but pay the most. Missed arrows scare nearby animals.
  6. Hold BREATH (button or Space) to steady your aim for a moment.
  7. Hits in a row build a combo. Each stage has a bonus objective that gives you an extra arrow.
  8. Earn coins and buy permanent upgrades. If you run out of arrows you can buy one more at a time with coins. You have 2 hearts: fail a stage and you lose one. Lose them all and the run is over.
