How to play

  1. Swipe up anywhere on the screen. A longer swipe throws a higher arc.
  2. Swipe a little left or right to aim. Let go to shoot.
  3. A clean swish = 3 points. A shot that touches the rim = 2.
  4. Streaks: 3 baskets in a row = x2, 6 = x3, 10 = x4. One miss resets it.
  5. The backboard square glows gold only while FAST x2 is on.
  6. Tap the small FAST x2 button and the hoop moves much faster. Score while it is on and you get x2 points. Tap again to turn it off.
  7. Online: create a match, send the 4-letter code to a friend, they join, and either of you taps Start. Highest score when time runs out wins.
