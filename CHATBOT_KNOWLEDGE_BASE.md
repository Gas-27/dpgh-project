## SERVICE INFORMATION

### Data Bundles
- **Description**: Instant data bundle purchases for MTN, AirtelTigo, and Telecel networks
- **Networks Supported**: MTN, AirtelTigo, Telecel
- **Delivery**: Instant - Data credited immediately after payment (within 30 seconds)
- **Availability**: 24/7
- **Support**: Available on WhatsApp and phone

### AFA (Agricultural Financing and Assurance)
- **Full Name**: Agricultural Financing and Assurance
- **What is AFA**: Government-backed agricultural insurance and financing program that provides farmers with subsidized packages and financial support
- **Benefits**:
  - Discounted data rates for farmers
  - Government subsidy on eligible packages
  - Easy registration process
  - Support for agricultural activities
- **Eligibility**: Must be a farmer or agricultural worker
- **Registration Time**: 15-30 minutes
- **Delivery Time**: 1-2 business days after approval
- **Required Information**:
  - Full name
  - Phone number
  - Date of birth
  - Region/Town
  - Occupation/Crop type

### Agent Program
- **Description**: Become a reseller and earn commissions on every sale
- **Commission Structure**:
  - **Starter**: 5% commission (for new agents starting out)
  - **Regular**: 7.5% commission (for agents with 100+ sales per month)
  - **Elite**: 10% commission (for agents with 500+ sales per month)
- **Requirements**:
  - Valid phone number
  - WhatsApp account
  - Willingness to serve customers
  - Must be 18+ years old
- **Benefits**:
  - Earn 5-10% commission on every sale
  - Priority customer support
  - Access to bulk discounts
  - Build your own customer base
  - Flexible working hours
  - No startup costs
- **Payment Methods for Payouts**: Mobile Money (MTN, AirtelTigo, Telecel), Bank Transfer, Wallet Top-up
- **Subagent Program**: Agents can create and manage subagent networks to expand reach and earn additional commissions from subagent sales

## WITHDRAWAL INFORMATION

### Requirements
- **Who Can Withdraw**: Only agents and subagents can withdraw. Regular customers cannot withdraw.

### Withdrawal Details
- **Minimum Withdrawal Amount**: GH₵16
- **Payment Methods**: Mobile Money, Bank Transfer, Wallet
- **Processing Time**: **Less than 2 minutes** (Money arrives almost instantly!)
- **Processing Time Detail**: Money reaches your account within 2 minutes after confirmation

### Withdrawal Steps
1. Go to Withdrawals page (for agents/subagents)
2. Enter the amount (minimum GH₵16)
3. Select payment method (Mobile Money, Bank Transfer, or Wallet)
4. Add recipient if not already saved
5. Confirm withdrawal
6. Receive money in less than 2 minutes

### Recipients
- **What is a Recipient**: Mobile money number or bank account details
- **Saving Recipients**: Save multiple recipients for faster withdrawals in the future
- **Limit**: Unlimited recipients can be saved
- **Benefits**: No need to re-enter details every time you withdraw

## AGENT PROGRAM DETAILS

### How to Become an Agent
1. Click 'Become an Agent' button on homepage
2. Fill out registration form with your details
3. Verify your phone number via OTP
4. Create your store/storefront
5. Wait for approval (usually 24-48 hours)
6. Start selling once approved

### Agent Profile Management
- **Storefront Theme**: Customize your storefront colors and branding
- **Pricing**: Set your own selling prices above base price
- **Subagents**: Create and manage subagent networks
- **Settings**: Manage WhatsApp, phone, notifications

### Subagents
- **Description**: Someone recruited by an agent to also sell data bundles
- **Benefits for Agents**:
  - Earn commission from subagent sales
  - Expand your network
  - Build a team
- **Subagent Commission**: Agents set subagent margins (varies by agent)
- **Sub-Subagents**: Subagents can also create their own sub-subagent networks (Multi-level network)

## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

### Payment Questions
1. **What payment methods do you accept?**
   - Mobile Money (MTN, AirtelTigo, Telecel), Bank Transfers, Card payments, Wallet top-ups

2. **Are transactions safe and secure?**
   - Yes, all transactions are encrypted and processed through secure payment gateways

3. **What if my payment fails?**
   - Check your balance and try again. If issues persist, contact our support team

4. **Do you accept international payments?**
   - Currently, we primarily serve customers in Ghana. International transactions may be limited

5. **Is there a transaction fee?**
   - Mobile money transactions may include network provider fees. Bank transfers have minimal fees

### Delivery Questions
1. **How long does data delivery take?**
   - Data is delivered instantly (within 30 seconds) after successful payment confirmation

2. **I paid but didn't receive my data**
   - Wait 5-10 minutes and refresh your account. If still not received, contact support immediately with your phone number

3. **Why is my delivery delayed?**
   - Delays can occur due to network issues, high traffic, or service provider problems. We investigate and resolve within 30 minutes

4. **Can I resend data to another phone number?**
   - Contact support with the incorrect number and correct number. We'll help transfer the balance

### Package Questions
1. **What networks do you support?**
   - MTN, AirtelTigo (Airtel), and Telecel (Vodafone) networks

2. **What data sizes are available?**
   - Packages from 100MB to 10GB+. Sizes vary by network and agent pricing

3. **Do unused data roll over?**
   - Data rollover depends on the specific package. Some packages keep unused data for 30 days. Check package details

4. **What is the validity of data packages?**
   - Most packages are valid for 30 days from activation. Check specific package terms for details

5. **Can I combine multiple packages?**
   - Yes, you can stack multiple packages. Each will have its own validity period

### AFA Questions
1. **What is AFA?**
   - AFA (Agricultural Financing and Assurance) is a government-backed program offering subsidized data for farmers

2. **How long does AFA registration take?**
   - Registration takes 15-30 minutes. Data delivery happens within 1-2 business days after approval

3. **Who is eligible for AFA?**
   - Farmers, agricultural workers, and people involved in farming activities are eligible

4. **What info do I need for AFA registration?**
   - Name, phone number, date of birth, region, town, and type of crop/occupation

5. **Are AFA packages cheaper?**
   - Yes, AFA packages include government subsidies, making them significantly cheaper than regular packages

### Agent Questions
1. **How much commission do agents earn?**
   - Commissions are 5% (starter), 7.5% (regular with 100+ sales), or 10% (elite with 500+ sales monthly)

2. **How long does it take to become an agent?**
   - Registration is instant, but approval takes 24-48 hours after verification

3. **Is there a startup cost?**
   - No startup costs. You can start selling immediately with zero upfront investment

4. **How do I withdraw my earnings?**
   - Go to your Withdrawals section, enter amount (minimum GH₵16), select payment method, and confirm. Money arrives in less than 2 minutes!

5. **What is the minimum withdrawal amount?**
   - The minimum withdrawal amount is GH₵16. Any amount below this cannot be withdrawn

6. **How long does withdrawal take?**
   - Withdrawals are processed in less than 2 minutes. Once you confirm, money reaches your account almost instantly

7. **Can I create subagents?**
   - Yes, agents can create and manage subagent networks to expand their reach and earn additional commissions

8. **What is a subagent?**
   - A subagent is someone recruited by an agent to also sell data bundles. The agent earns commission from their subagent's sales

### Support Questions
1. **How can I contact support?**
   - Contact us via WhatsApp for instant support (usually responds within 5 minutes) or call our support number

2. **What are support hours?**
   - We provide 24/7 support for all urgent issues. Response times are usually within 5-15 minutes

3. **How do I report a problem?**
   - Use the 'Report Complaint' option in the app, or contact support directly via WhatsApp with details

4. **What if I want to cancel an order?**
   - Once payment is confirmed and data is delivered, cancellation is not possible. Contact support for special cases

### Technical Questions
1. **The app keeps crashing**
   - Clear your app cache, update to the latest version, and restart your phone. Contact support if issue persists

2. **I can't log in**
   - Ensure correct phone number/email and password. Use 'Forgot Password' to reset if needed

3. **Prices are not showing**
   - Refresh the page, check internet connection, and clear browser cache. Try a different browser if it continues
